### About DSPMU

##### About the University

**About Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University**

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University (DSPMU), Ranchi, carries a distinguished legacy of nearly 185 years, tracing its institutional origins to 1839. Beginning as a Government English School through the initiative of Major J. R. Ousley, the then British Commissioner of Chhota Nagpur, the institution evolved through successive stages of academic expansion—from Ranchi Zila School in 1875 and an Intermediate College affiliated to Patna University, to Ranchi Government Degree College in 1946. …

## Admissions 2026-27

### Process

UG and PG applications are made through the Chancellor Portal (https://jharkhanduniversities.nic.in). The university publishes the counselling schedule, document checklist and current admission notices as PDFs linked from /news/admission-merit-list. The current site's fee and examination-notice HTML pages are empty; use the linked PDFs.

### Eligibility

The public pages reviewed for this build do not publish a consolidated, programme-specific eligibility table. Eligibility is programme-specific; confirm it with the department or admission office before applying. Check the current admission notice for CUET or other programme requirements.

### Documents required for UG admission

- 1. Chancellor’s portal Application Form

- 2. Two (02) passport Size photographs

- 3. Secondary school (class 10th) Mark sheet or equivalent

- 4. Pass Certificate of Class 10th or equivalent

- 5. Senior secondary school (class 12th) Mark Sheet or equivalent

- 6. Pass Certificate of Class 12th or equivalent

- 7. Aadhar Card

- 8. CUET Score Card (if applicable)

- 9. Character certificate

- 10. Migration Certificate

- 11. Transfer Certificate/College Leaving Certificate

- 12. Caste certificates (ST/SC/OBC -NCL/EWS) certificate (if applicable, must be issued by the competent authority)

- 13. PWD/ PH certificate (for differently abled candidates, if applicable, issued by the competent authority)

- 14. Certificate(s) for Representing International, National, or State Level in sports/Games ( if applicable, for candidates applying under the sports Quota)

- 15. BPL Certificate (if applicable, issued by Block Development Officer)

- 16. APAAR ID
