# DSPMU Site Knowledge

> Draft orientation for the admissions agent. Fees and dates must be confirmed with the university. The prompt carries the common facts and recent notices; the full fact and notice indexes are maintained outside it.

## University

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University (DSPMU), Ranchi, Jharkhand.

Source: https://dspmuranchi.ac.in/cms/about-the-university

## Admissions 2026-27

UG and PG applications are made through the Chancellor Portal (https://jharkhanduniversities.nic.in). Eligibility is programme-specific; the public pages reviewed do not publish a consolidated eligibility table. The document checklist and UG counselling dates are in the core cards.

Source: https://dspmuranchi.ac.in/news/admission-merit-list

## Fees 2026-27

The 2026 fee sheet covers 43 UG programmes. Annual fee: ₹3,170–₹4,975 (GEN), ₹2,945–₹4,300 (ST/SC/BC I & II). Semester course fee: ₹0–₹18,000 (GEN), depending on programme. Common programmes are in the core cards; if a programme is not listed, say the fee needs confirmation and give the registrar contact.

Source: https://dspmuranchi.ac.in/uploads/news/gallery/1785318304_034b0538cc479c48018a.pdf. Valid 2026-07-01 to 2027-06-30.
