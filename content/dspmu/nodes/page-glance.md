## Vision & Mission

The driving purpose, institutional values, and educational roadmap of DSPMU

### Institutional Vision

To establish Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University as a globally acknowledged seat of academic excellence, multidisciplinary research, and indigenous knowledge preservation, empowering diverse youth of Jharkhand and the nation with holistic wisdom, scientific temperament, and ethical leadership.

### Institutional Mission

- **Quality Education:** Impart accessible, value-based, and outcome-oriented higher education across Arts, Science, Social Sciences, Commerce, and Vocational streams.

- **Indigenous Linguistic Archival:** Promote, preserve, and conduct cutting-edge research in 9 indigenous tribal and regional languages of Jharkhand.

- **Skill & Employability:** Bridge academia-industry gaps through vocational training, MoUs, live projects, and professional entrepreneurship.

- **Social Empowerment:** Foster constitutional values, gender equality, environmental consciousness, and community service through NSS and NCC.

## Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

###### Central Library

Vast repository of books, national/international journals, e-books, INFLIBNET consortia, and digital reading halls.

###### Advanced Laboratories

Modern scientific laboratories with specialized instrumentation for physics, chemistry, biosciences, and geology research.

###### Hostel Accommodation

Separate secure residential hostel facilities for male and female scholars with mess, Wi-Fi, and 24/7 security.

###### Sports & Athletics

Located in the vibrant Morabadi sports hub with playgrounds, indoor games, gymnasium, and athletic coaching.

###### Smart Wi-Fi Campus

High-speed campus-wide fiber internet connectivity, smart multimedia classrooms, and virtual learning systems.

###### Placement & Training Cell

Active corporate linkage, campus recruitments, soft skills training, mock interviews, and career counseling sessions.

###### NCC & NSS Units

Active units with 20 NSS activities and 15 NCC activities fostering leadership and social service.

###### Grievance & Redressal

Online grievance redressal system, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), Anti-Ragging Cell, and Equal Opportunity Cell.
