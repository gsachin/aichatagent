## About the University

**About Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University**

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University (DSPMU), Ranchi, carries a distinguished legacy of nearly 185 years, tracing its institutional origins to 1839. Beginning as a Government English School through the initiative of Major J. R. Ousley, the then British Commissioner of Chhota Nagpur, the institution evolved through successive stages of academic expansion—from Ranchi Zila School in 1875 and an Intermediate College affiliated to Patna University, to Ranchi Government Degree College in 1946. Rechristened as Ranchi College in 1949, it became a constituent college of Bihar University in 1952, with postgraduate teaching commencing in 1951–52.

A landmark transformation came in 2018, when Ranchi College was upgraded to **Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University**, a State Unitary University under the RUSA (Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan) Programme. The transition marked not merely an institutional reconfiguration, but the continuation of a long and distinguished academic tradition committed to excellence in higher education, research, innovation and societal advancement.

Located amidst the verdant environs of Ranchi, DSPMU provides a vibrant and intellectually stimulating campus for nearly 16,000 students. Its facilities include a playground, boys’ hostel, Wi-Fi-enabled campus and sophisticated laboratories supporting contemporary teaching, research and innovation. The University offers 29 traditional and regular undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, besides 12 postgraduate and 4 certificate programmes in vocational studies, providing a broad and inclusive academic spectrum.

Situated within a distinctive socio-cultural milieu shaped substantially by tribal and indigenous traditions, the University bears a special responsibility towards the educational, intellectual and social advancement of the region. Its academic ethos is directed towards fostering critical inquiry, creativity, intellectual growth and responsible leadership. With nearly 95 per cent of its faculty members holding doctoral degrees, DSPMU remains committed to sustaining high standards of teaching, research and academic excellence.

In accordance with the directions of His Excellency the Governor-cum-Chancellor of the State Universities of Jharkhand, the University is scheduled to convene its Third Convocation Ceremony on 26 September 2026, with His Excellency the Governor-cum-Chancellor as the Chief Guest. The University is in the process of approaching persons of national and international eminence, distinguished for their exceptional contributions to academia, public policy, science, research and national development, with a view to inviting an eminent personality of exceptional standing as the Guest of Honour. Arrangements are being made to ensure  the solemn and dignified conduct of the third Convocation of the University.
