Four Year UG program Admission 2026-27 
List of Documents Required during Counselling cum Admission process 
The applicants must produce the following documents in original along with one set of self - 
attested photocopies for each program at the respective department.  
1. Chancellor’s portal Application Form  
2. Two (02) passport Size photographs  
3. Secondary school (class 10th) Mark sheet or equivalent  
4. Pass Certificate of Class 10th or equivalent  
5. Senior secondary school (class 12th) Mark Sheet or equivalent  
6. Pass Certificate of Class 12th or equivalent 
7. Aadhar Card 
8. CUET Score Card (if applicable) 
9. Character certificate  
10. Migration Certificate 
11.Transfer Certificate/College Leaving Certificate  
12. Caste certificates (ST/SC/OBC -NCL/EWS) certificate (if applicable, must be issued by 
the competent authority)  
13. PWD/ PH certificate (for differently abled  candidates, if applicable, issued by the 
competent authority)  
14. Certificate(s) for Representing International, National, or State Level in sports/Games ( if 
applicable, for candidates applying under the sports Quota)  
15. BPL Certificate (if applicable, issued by Block Development Officer) 
16. APAAR ID
