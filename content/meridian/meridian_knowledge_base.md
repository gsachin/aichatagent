## University Overview

Meridian University is an independent, coeducational institution offering undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs across six schools. Founded in 1978 on a 120-acre campus, Meridian educates over 5,000 students from more than 30 countries.

- **Motto / Tagline:** "Inspiring Tomorrow's Leaders"
- **Approach:** rigorous academics paired with direct exposure to industry, research and community.

### Mission

To deliver an education that is rigorous, relevant and accessible, preparing students to lead with competence and integrity.

### Vision

To be recognized among the world's most trusted institutions for producing graduates who shape the industries they enter.

### Core Values

- **Academic Integrity** — rigorous, transparent standards in research, teaching and assessment.
- **Real-World Relevance** — curricula built and reviewed alongside the industries graduates enter.
- **Inclusive Community** — students and faculty from every background given equal footing to contribute and lead.
- **Responsible Innovation** — new ideas, technologies and methods pursued with accountability.

## Admission Overview

Meridian accepts applications for undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs across two intake cycles each year (Fall and Spring, with limited Summer options for select programs). Admission is based on academic record, entrance scores where applicable, and — for select programs — a personal statement or interview.

### Eligibility

- **Undergraduate:** Completion of 10+2 (or equivalent) with the minimum subject requirements for the chosen program.
- **Postgraduate:** A relevant bachelor's degree with minimum aggregate marks as specified per program.
- **Doctoral:** A relevant master's degree, a research proposal, and faculty availability in the chosen area.

### Admission Process

1. **Step 1 — Apply Online:** Complete the application form with academic and personal details.
2. **Step 2 — Submit Documents:** Upload transcripts, identification and any required test scores.
3. **Step 3 — Admission Review:** The admissions committee reviews the complete application profile.
4. **Step 4 — Offer Letter:** Applicant receives the admission decision and instructions to enroll.

### Required Documents

- Academic transcripts and mark sheets from the most recent qualification.
- Government-issued photo ID or passport.
- Recent passport-size photograph as per application specifications.
- Statement of Purpose (required for postgraduate and doctoral applications).
- International applicants: valid passport copy and, where applicable, an English proficiency score.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I apply to more than one program?**
Yes, applicants may apply to up to two programs within the same intake cycle.

**Is there an application fee?**
A nominal non-refundable processing fee applies per application, as listed in the Fees Structure section.

**Do international students need additional documents?**
International applicants should also submit a valid passport copy and, where applicable, an English proficiency score.

**When will I hear back after applying?**
Admission decisions are typically released within 4–6 weeks of the application deadline.

**How much is the tuition for the MBA program?** *(derived from the Postgraduate Programs table)*
The Master of Business Administration (MBA) is a 2-year program with tuition of $18,500 per year. Eligibility: a Bachelor's Degree.

**What intakes does Meridian offer?** *(derived from Admission Overview)*
Meridian accepts applications across two intake cycles each year: Fall and Spring, with limited Summer options for select programs.

**What are the application deadlines?** *(derived from Important Dates)*
The early application deadline is January 15 and the regular application deadline is April 30. Admission decisions are released on June 10 and the Fall semester begins on August 1.

**How do I apply to Meridian?** *(derived from Admission Process)*
Apply online with the application form, submit documents (transcripts, ID, test scores), pass the admission review, and receive the offer letter with enrollment instructions.
