**MERIDIAN UNIVERSITY**

**Student Admissions – Answered FAQ**

**Fees, Scholarships & Financial Assistance**

**1\. Why are the fees so high?**

Meridian’s annual undergraduate tuition ranges from $7,900 to $15,200, while postgraduate tuition ranges from $9,600 to $18,500. The reference also lists facilities and learning resources such as smart classrooms, a central library, innovation labs, sports facilities and residence halls. The document does not provide a detailed fee-comparison or justification for why the fees are considered high. Our placement ratio is 95%.

**2\. Can I pay the fees in installments instead of paying the full amount at once?**

Yes. Fees may be paid annually or split into two semester installments. A late payment fee applies after the published due date each semester.

**3\. Does Meridian offer scholarships?**

Yes. Meridian offers Merit Scholarships of up to 50% tuition waiver for top-scoring incoming students, as well as Sports & Arts Scholarships for students with distinguished sporting or artistic achievement.

**4\. Does Meridian provide any fee discounts or financial aid?**

Meridian provides need-based financial aid for students demonstrating economic need. The reference document does not specify general fee discounts beyond the listed scholarships and aid.

**5\. Is the application fee mandatory?**

A nominal, non-refundable processing fee applies per application. The listed fee is $60 for undergraduate applications and $75 for postgraduate applications.
