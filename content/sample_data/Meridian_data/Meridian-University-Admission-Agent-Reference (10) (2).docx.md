## **2.9 Admission Overview**

Meridian accepts applications for undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs across two intake cycles each year (Fall and Spring, with limited Summer options for select programs). Admission is based on academic record, entrance scores where applicable, and — for select programs — a personal statement or interview.

### **Eligibility**

* Undergraduate: Completion of 10+2 (or equivalent) with the minimum subject requirements for the chosen program.

* Postgraduate: A relevant bachelor's degree with minimum aggregate marks as specified per program.

* Doctoral: A relevant master's degree, a research proposal, and faculty availability in the chosen area.

### **Admission Process**

* Step 1 — Apply Online: Complete the application form with academic and personal details.

* Step 2 — Submit Documents: Upload transcripts, identification and any required test scores.

* Step 3 — Admission Review: The admissions committee reviews the complete application profile.

* Step 4 — Offer Letter: Applicant receives the admission decision and instructions to enroll.

### **Required Documents**

* Academic transcripts and mark sheets from the most recent qualification.

* Government-issued photo ID or passport.

* Recent passport-size photograph as per application specifications.

* Statement of Purpose (required for postgraduate and doctoral applications).

* International applicants: valid passport copy and, where applicable, an English proficiency score.

## **2.13 Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Can I apply to more than one program?**

Yes, applicants may apply to up to two programs within the same intake cycle.

### **Is there an application fee?**

A nominal non-refundable processing fee applies per application, as listed in the Fees Structure section.

### **Do international students need additional documents?**

International applicants should also submit a valid passport copy and, where applicable, an English proficiency score.

### **When will I hear back after applying?**

Admission decisions are typically released within 4–6 weeks of the application deadline.
