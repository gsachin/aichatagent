---
Before you start

Make sure Ollama 🦙 is running in your system tray. If not, open it from the Start Menu.

---
Step 1: Open first terminal

Press Win + R, type cmd, hit Enter.

cd D:\university_project_demo
streamlit run app.py

Wait until you see: You can now view your Streamlit app in your browser.

---
Step 2: Open second terminal

Press Win + R, type cmd, hit Enter (keep the first one running).

"C:\Program Files (x86)\cloudflared\cloudflared.exe" tunnel --url http://localhost:8501

---
Step 3: Get the public URL

In the second terminal, look for this line:

|  Your quick Tunnel has been created! Visit it at:
|  https://xxxxxxxx.trycloudflare.com

---
Step 4: Share the URL

Copy that https://xxxxxxxx.trycloudflare.com link and share it. Anyone with the link can use your chatbot from anywhere.

---
Step 5: Stop

When done, press Ctrl+C in both terminals.
